ATOM FX · Screen Kit

Every surface, and how they connect

A visual reference for Claude Code: all screens — the pair Energy Wheel, the new Currency Strength Wheel, Macro, Insights — plus every bottom sheet, the simple line chart, and Settings. Navigation is a 4-tab bottom bar you can swipe between; detail rises in bottom sheets. State shown is the spec's canonical Risk-On example.

Skip to the Flow map at the bottom for the plain-English "tap X → see Y" walkthrough.

01

Top-level screens · bottom nav + swipe

Four destinations: Wheel · Currency · Macro · Insights. Swipe left/right or tap the nav. Settings lives behind the header gear.

Dark Wheel — landing
The pair Energy Wheel. Tap a node → pair sheet · tap a ring → factor sheet · tap the nucleus → regime/macro.
Dark Currency — strength wheel NEW
8 currencies, radius = strength. Risk bloc on top, defensive on bottom — a top-heavy bloom reads "risk-on" instantly.
Dark Macro — archetype
The named regime + currency bias + evidence axes (anti double-count), then the full cross-asset dashboard.
Dark Insights — recommendation, news, calendar
The AI synthesis, theme-tagged headlines, and upcoming events in one scroll.
Light Wheel
Both wheels shown in light too — every surface is theme-aware; glow becomes soft shadow on paper.
Light Currency
Tap a currency → its detail sheet, and its pairs highlight when you swipe back to the Wheel.
02

Bottom sheets · one tap deep

Rise above any screen; the wheel stays visible behind at half-height. Pair sheet + the six factor sheets.

Dark Pair sheet — WHY + line charts
Tap a node. The three line charts (D1/H4/H1) sit up top; the six-factor WHY shows exactly where the pair stopped.
Dark Regime sheet
Tap ring 1 (or the nucleus). D1/H4/H1 regime, the three votes, stability, and the macro-archetype link.
Dark Currency Flow sheet
Tap ring 2. Strength vs Δ; current leader (flow) vs absolute leader — the distinction the wheel is built on.
Dark Breadth sheet
Tap ring 3. Is the move broad or from one pair? Bar meters + support/total + band.
Dark Momentum sheet
Tap ring 4. MOM1212 D1/H4/H1 + deltas, CMP + status, and a sparkline.
Dark Structure sheet
Tap ring 5. D1/H4 direction, last BOS/CHoCH event, strength. A counter-CHoCH is a red warning.
Dark Entry sheet
Tap ring 6. "Should I enter now?" — setup rank, continuation, ADX, reset, ATR%, alignment, EMA200, verdict.
Dark Currency detail sheet
Tap a currency node. CSM across D1/H4/H1, breadth, its handbook drivers, and the pairs that express it.
Dark Settings
Header gear. Theme, notifications, data source, optional GitHub PAT. No market/AI keys needed on the device.

04

Reading the wheels

Pair wheel — radius = potential

Angle is fixed identity (EURUSD always 12 o'clock). A node earns its way outward by passing each ring in order. Colour = long/short. Dots = which factors passed.

Currency wheel — radius = strength

8 currencies at fixed positions; risk bloc top, defensive bottom. Outer = strong, centre = weak. A top-heavy bloom = risk-on; bottom-heavy = risk-off. Δ chevrons show who's turning.

Both are linked

Tap a currency → its pairs highlight on the pair wheel. A currency bloom and a pair-arc bloom are the same market theme, seen two ways — and the Macro screen names it.

Breadth keeps it honest

A bloom's confidence is tied to breadth, so one strongly-moving pair can't fake a broad theme (the handbook's double-counting rule).
